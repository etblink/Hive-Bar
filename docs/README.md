## Historical milestone records

Files named for M1 through M16 and completed M17 submilestones are preserved as contemporaneous specifications, runbooks, or evidence. They may accurately say that a then-future action was unexecuted, write-disabled, or incomplete. Do not rewrite those historical facts merely to make them sound current.

When a historical runbook conflicts with `PRODUCTION_OPERATIONS.md`, the current operations document governs present procedure unless a newer accepted milestone explicitly supersedes it.
